About Me

Jacky Wu

大家好，我是 Jacky Wu，也就是玩AI的杰克。我曾任樊登读书企业版技术负责人和 IBM 架构师，有 20 年技术开发与企业数字化实践。

Hi, I’m Jacky Wu, also known as 玩AI的杰克. I previously led technology for Fandeng Reading’s enterprise edition and worked as an architect at IBM. I have 20 years of experience in software development and enterprise digital transformation.

这个博客里有我早年写下的 Java、Linux、数据库和 Web 开发笔记。现在，我也在这里记录怎么用 AI 做实验，怎么把软件工程的方法用到产品和运营里。写到一个方法时，我会尽量交代当时遇到了什么问题、具体怎么做，以及哪些地方还没验证清楚。

This blog includes some of my earlier notes on Java, Linux, databases, and web development. These days, I also write about my AI experiments and how I apply software engineering to product development and operations. Whenever I write about a method, I try to explain the problem I faced, what I did, and what I still haven’t verified.

在家育种业工作时，我参与了生产和育种数据的整理、分析与报表工作。刚接触这个行业的时候，我以为把系统和数据理顺就能解决大部分问题。真正往下做，才发现得先弄懂现场怎么记录、指标怎么算，以及这些数据能回答什么问题。那段时间，我补了数量遗传学和统计方面的基础，也用 R 和 Python 跑了一些例子，把学习过程整理成了一份双语自学教程，分享给同样从计算机背景入门的朋友。

During my time at BGG Group, I helped organize and analyze production and breeding data and prepare reports. When I entered the industry, I thought getting the systems and data in order would solve most problems. Once I got into the work, I realized I first needed to understand how people recorded data in their daily work, how they calculated the metrics, and what questions the data could answer. I studied the basics of quantitative genetics and statistics, worked through examples in R and Python, and turned that learning process into a bilingual self-study guide that I shared with others coming from a computing background.

我也在运营「玩AI的杰克」的公众号和视频号。公众号适合把一次工具实践写透，视频号则让我练习在几分钟里讲清楚一个技术问题。我把生成式 AI 用到选题、写稿、配图、配音和字幕里，也用它做小工具原型、整理可以重复使用的工作流。这些实践让我慢慢摸清了哪些环节可以交给 AI，哪些还得自己回看。像文章里的出处、教程里的操作步骤、成片里的字幕，我都会按最终版本再过一遍。

I also run the 玩AI的杰克 WeChat Official Account and WeChat Channels account. On the Official Account, I can write in depth about trying out a tool. On Channels, I practice explaining a technical problem in a few minutes. I use generative AI to develop topics, draft articles, make illustrations, create voiceovers, and produce subtitles. I also use it to prototype small tools and document reusable workflows. That work has helped me see which steps I can hand off to AI and which still need my own review. I check article sources, tutorial steps, and subtitles against the final versions.
